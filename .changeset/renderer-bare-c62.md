A quantity in unit C62 prints as the bare number (`4`, not `4 Unitless` / `4 Ohne Einheit`), also as a price's base quantity (`per 100`). C62 is a count with no unit of measure; its codelist label describes the code for a picker and stays as it is. With nothing printed, the unit has no mark of its own: a highlighted finding on it outlines the line.
